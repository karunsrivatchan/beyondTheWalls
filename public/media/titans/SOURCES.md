## Dossier portraits and resolution

Dossiers fit artwork inside the left pane, anchored to its bottom-left corner, without stretching beyond the source width or clipping wide faces. They serve the original local files directly to avoid another lossy optimization pass. The gallery retains its full-body cutouts.

- `female-original.png`: the artist's original 442 × 1392 transparent PNG, downloaded from the same [Mauri7super artwork](https://www.deviantart.com/mauri7super/art/Female-Titan-Full-Render-1124101820), replacing the smaller 318 × 995 version in dossiers.
- Colossal dossiers use the existing 1920 × 1080 `public/media/colossal-close.jpg` anime still rather than enlarging the 200px-wide gallery cutout.
- `founding-portrait.webp`: 1201 × 613 anime still, compressed at quality 95 from `qa/founder-mappa-reference.png`. [Source article and image](https://comicbook.com/anime/news/attack-on-titan-graphic-how-big-is-eren-final-form/). Used only in the dossier to show the face clearly; the gallery's full-body model illustration remains separate.

## Beast Titan extraction

Built-in image tool, background-extraction edit, saved locally as `beast-sticker.webp`.

Final prompt: “Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the attached full-body Beast Titan render. Remove only the solid pale background to create a clean full-body sticker cutout on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Preserve this exact Titan, face, pose, fur, anatomy, colors, hands and feet. Do not redesign, crop, add objects, add text, or add an outline. Keep the complete body with a little clear padding.”

## Expanded full-body catalogue

The displayed catalogue contains 18 distinct forms. Nine additional local transparent illustrations were created with the built-in image tool during this task: `rod-reiss`, `ymir-founder`, `ymir-jaw`, `falco-jaw`, `falco-flying`, `ksaver-beast`, `falco-pure`, `comrade-d`, and `crawling` (each with the `-sticker.webp` suffix). They are illustrated reconstructions, not official anime renders or photographs; the page and dossiers disclose this. The original generation prompts are retained in the task's tool history. The similar Kruger entry was removed, and Grisha's unsatisfactory illustration was excluded when a suitable full-body replacement cutout could not be sourced. The user's earlier preference was to omit forms without a suitable full image. Attempted image-tool pose edits and extractions were rejected and are not used.

Only complete bodies are displayed. Head-and-shoulder extractions, Uri's incomplete manifestation, unavailable forms, and uncertain depictions are excluded. Similar Colossal and Galliard illustrations are consolidated into their existing entries with known holders in the dossiers. The extra unused assets remain unreferenced by the page.

The Founding Titan occupies a full-width stage beside a much smaller Colossal reference. This emphasizes its enormous scale; no precise height is asserted for its skeletal form. Gallery dimensions are adapted to the screen, not a literal scale chart.
